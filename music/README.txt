ROOM SONGS
==========
Put the room's royalty-free lofi songs here (mp3, ogg or 16-bit wav).

* When the room starts, one of the songs in this folder plays at random.
* The music player on the PC opens in this folder. If you browse somewhere else,
  the room remembers that folder next time (browse back here to go back to the default).
* Only use songs you have the right to share, and keep their licence info
  (for example in a credits file next to them).
