# Lofi Room 1.0: what ships

Frozen on November 11, 2026. Launch: free on itch.io (pay what you want, $0 allowed), around December 15.

1.0 is the room as it is now, fixed, polished and tested on real headsets. **Nothing new goes in from here.**
A new idea goes on the "After 1.0" list at the bottom, however small. Between now and launch the work is
only: fixing bugs, speed, security, licences, the release builds and the itch.io page.

A change belongs in 1.0 only if it does one of these:
- fixes a crash, a stuck state or something that doesn't work as this list says;
- makes something run fast enough on a target headset;
- closes a security hole or a licence problem;
- is needed to build, install, update or explain the room.


## Ships in 1.0

### Where it runs
- Windows PC: flat screen (mouse and keyboard, or a gamepad) and PC VR (Steam Frame, or a Quest through Steam Link).
- Steam Frame: its own build, once tested on the headset.
- Meta Quest 3 standalone: **only if it is tested on a real Quest 3 before launch**; otherwise it moves to 1.1.
  On the Quest the consoles are off (they need Android builds of every emulator).

### The apartment
- Bedroom, hallway with the wall phone and front door, living room with the Big TV, game room with the CRT,
  VCR and console, theater, study, the hidden Noir office, and the balcony over the street.
- Walking, turning, sitting (beanbags, the swivelling recliner), close-up views (V on desktop, B or Y in VR).
- The room remembers where you left off.

### Living in it
- Music through the whole flat from the PC, with a speaker bank that can play a different source per room.
- Weather (clear, rain, snow, storm), blackout curtains, lamps and lights.
- The cats, the cat photo frames, incense, the record player, napping and making the bed.
- Books and comics (ebooks, .cbz and folders of images) you can carry and read; the diary of what you read and played.
- The poster and the screens: your own pictures and GIFs on the poster, TV and monitor.

### Video
- The VCR on the CRT, the Big TV and the Theater Screen, each with its own player (VLC built in: mkv, mp4, avi, mov, webm and more).
- Picture shape, subtitle and audio tracks, the channel knob's picture styles, thumbs up / down ratings.

### Games
- The cart console with 8-BIT, 16-BIT, POCKET, BLAST 16 and ARCADE carts, and the DISC and MINI DISC discs
  (NES, SNES, Game Boy family, Genesis family, Neo Geo, PlayStation, GameCube). Windows and Linux PCs; on the Steam
  Frame they depend on its build, which the headset tests settle.
- **Players bring their own emulators and games.** The download ships no cores and no games; `cores/CORES.txt` says
  which file goes where.
- Generic cart names and our own cart art; players can rename any cart (`label.txt`) and put their own picture on it (`art.png`).
- Two controllers and two light guns that land somewhere new on the rug each visit, input switching by itself
  when a gun is picked up, button remapping for gamepad, keyboard and VR controllers.
- Games keep their place (save states) and battery saves.
- The arcade cabinet, the plain cart's built-in demo game.

### Multiplayer
- The wall phone: invite codes, up to 8 players, host approval before guests join, guest-control settings.
- Voice chat with mute and push-to-talk; shared music, shared TVs and shared console games; shared books.
- A relay for players whose networks block direct links (the room server on the current droplet).

### Events and easter eggs
- The white T-Rex, King Gaboon, the fight in the city and the breaking news on every TV.
- The UFO, the Konami-code tornado, Noir Night and its cases, the secret cart.

### Keeping it up to date
- The signed in-game updater (PC) carries every update after the first install from itch.io.


## Known limits at 1.0

These are true at launch and are not bugs to fix before it:
- The consoles are certain to work only on PCs. On the Steam Frame they work only if its build can run the
  emulator (headset testing settles it), and on the Quest 3 not at all at launch.
- GameCube needs a graphics card with OpenGL 3.3 or newer.
- The in-game updater is PC only; Quest players (if the Quest makes it) update by downloading the new file.
- Multiplayer needs the room server to be up; single-player works without it.


## After 1.0

Parked for 1.1 or later. Anything new goes here.
- The Quest 3 standalone version, if it doesn't make launch.
- Consoles on the Quest (Android builds of every core).
- Consoles on the Steam Frame, if the Frame build can't run them at launch.
- A Patreon page for supporters.
- A Steam release.
- The rooftop.
- The buddy kaiju swooping in after the final blow.
- New consoles, rooms or events.
